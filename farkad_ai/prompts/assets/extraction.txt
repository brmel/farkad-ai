Extract every {title} entry from what you are given, and nothing else.

{guidance}

What you are given is the part of a log that is {title}, one thing per line, in the words
the log used. Return one entry for each line — two only where a line is really two things.
An entry is that thing alone: its name and its amounts come from its own line.

The whole log, for what it says about when or how — never as more things to record, because
the rest of it is recorded elsewhere and recording it here would count it twice:
{log}

Leave a field out rather than inventing a value for it; a missing field is honest, a
guessed one is not.

A word the user actually said is not a guess. Read it onto the field's own scale: a
field described as 1 poor to 5 excellent has a value for "badly", and a stated size or
intensity changes the amount you would otherwise have assumed — "a big glass" is more
than a glass. A count of servings is a stated amount too: "one scoop", "a dose", "two
capsules" is that many of the product's usual serving, so estimate the amount from it.
Omitting is for what the user left unsaid, not for what they said without a number.
